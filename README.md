# 🎬 Favorite Series: Breaking Bad

> *"I am not in danger, Skyler. I am the danger."* — Walter White

---

## 📌 Overview
**Breaking Bad** is an American crime drama television series created by *Vince Gilligan*. Set and shot in Albuquerque, New Mexico, the series tells the story of **Walter White**, an underpaid and dispirited high school chemistry teacher who turns to a life of crime.

---

| Character | Role | Key Trait |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Jesse Pinkman** | Partner | Loyalty & Heart |
| **Hank Schrader** | DEA Agent | Relentless Pursuit |
| **Gus Fring** | Drug Lord | Professional Precision |

## ⚡ Why It's Worth Watching

1. **Character Arc:** Incredible evolution from mild-mannered teacher to ruthless kingpin.
2. **Writing & Cinematography:** Top-tier storytelling, symbolism, and visual framing.
3. **Pacing:** Gripping suspense that keeps you engaged from start to finish.

---

## 💡 Key Highlights & Themes

- `Chemistry`: The science of change and transformation.
- `Morality`: How desperation tests ethical boundaries.
- `Consequences`: Every action triggers an unavoidable chain reaction.
